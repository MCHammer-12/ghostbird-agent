The best customer relationships aren’t built when everything goes right.

They’re built in the moments when you have to tell someone something they don’t want to hear.

When we switched inventory systems, we had a two-day window where both systems were half-working. We almost shipped an order to the wrong facility.

We caught it. Then we told our top customers what was happening before they found out the hard way.

Two of them wrote back just to say thanks for the heads up.

My dad always said, “Bad news doesn’t get better with age.” I finally understand what he meant.
