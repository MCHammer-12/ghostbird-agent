# Ghostbird research

Public research completed August 29, 2026.

## Positioning

Ghostbird describes itself as a LinkedIn ghostwriting agency focused on audience growth, trust, brand recognition, and inbound leads. LinkedIn lists the company as founded in 2024 with 11-50 employees.

Source: [Ghostbird on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ghostbird/)

## Publicly described workflow

Founder Ryan Cook describes the process as:

1. Run a content interview every month or two.
2. Arrive with industry research, proven formats, timely topics, and competitor angles.
3. Interview the client to pull out the details needed for posts, at roughly five minutes per post.
4. Draft posts and images using agency frameworks.
5. Assign posts to different funnel roles: reach new people, build trust, or convert.
6. Let the client review, edit, and approve before publishing.

Source: [Ryan Cook's description of Ghostbird's process](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ryan-travis-cook_hiring-a-ghostwriter-sounds-a-little-sketchy-activity-7485382624958410753-b0_U)

## Writer-quality signals

In a public post about hiring ghostwriters, Ryan says winning transcript-to-post samples had strong hooks, short paragraphs, effective transitions, satisfying story arcs, client-industry research, accessible reading level, and useful images or graphics.

Source: [Ryan Cook on evaluating ghostwriters](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ryan-travis-cook_two-weeks-ago-i-hired-the-top-5-ghostwriters-activity-7332902429006471168-hPRP)

## Product implications

- Treat interviews as a primary content asset and preserve question-and-answer boundaries.
- Allow one interview to produce multiple linked post ideas.
- Support pre-interview research and post-interview extraction as separate workflows.
- Store a post's intended funnel role or business goal.
- Keep agency frameworks configurable rather than baking one writing method into the product.
- Support image and asset recommendations tied to a specific story or post idea.
- Preserve client edits and approvals because Ghostbird already treats iteration as part of learning voice and strategy.
- Evaluate the synthetic dataset on the same transcript-to-post skills Ghostbird publicly uses when hiring writers.
